# MRI Safety QuickCheck Support

For app support, account help, subscription questions, or to report an MRI data issue, email dballas88@gmail.com.

Include the app version and build number shown in Settings. For MRI data issues, include the manufacturer, device family or model, selected scanner field strength, result shown, and the public manufacturer source link when available.

Do not include patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, accession numbers, images, or other patient-identifying information.
