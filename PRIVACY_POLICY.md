# MRI Safety QuickCheck Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 6, 2026**

MRI Safety QuickCheck is a clinical decision-support application intended for MRI personnel. This privacy policy describes how information is handled in the controlled beta version of the app.

## Information we collect

MRI Safety QuickCheck uses Supabase Authentication to create and manage user accounts. Account information may include the email address used to register and authentication/session data required to keep the user signed in.

The application may also store user-created app data associated with an account, such as saved scanner profiles, favorites, recent-device activity, and QuickCheck history needed to support the application workflow.

## Patient information

The controlled beta is not intended for entry of patient-identifying information. Do not enter patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, accession numbers, images, or other protected health information into feedback, support messages, or free-text fields.

## MRI and device information

Device, scanner, and compatibility information entered into the app is used to provide the requested decision-support workflow and related history. MRI Safety QuickCheck is not a substitute for current manufacturer MRI labeling, institutional policy, or qualified MRI personnel review.

## Authentication and local storage

Authentication is provided through Supabase Auth. On supported iOS devices, authentication session data is stored using Expo SecureStore, which uses platform-protected secure storage such as the iOS Keychain.

## Feedback and support

If a user chooses to send beta feedback or report an MRI data issue, the application opens the user's email client with a prefilled message. The user controls whether the message is sent. Feedback templates instruct users not to include patient-identifying information.

## Crash and analytics data

The controlled beta does not intentionally include third-party crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. Platform providers such as Apple, Expo, and Supabase may process technical information according to their own services and privacy terms when those services are used.

## Advertising and tracking

MRI Safety QuickCheck does not intentionally use data for cross-app advertising or tracking in the controlled beta.

## Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with service providers only as necessary to operate the application, such as authentication, backend database, build/distribution, and platform services.

## Data retention and account deletion

Account and application data may be retained while the controlled beta is active and as needed for security, quality, troubleshooting, and regulatory or legal obligations. A production release must provide a clear account-deletion request path before public App Store launch if account creation remains available in the app.

## Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect account and application data. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

## Children

MRI Safety QuickCheck is intended for professional clinical users and is not directed to children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the application, service providers, or beta program change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
